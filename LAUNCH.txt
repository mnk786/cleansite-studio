CleanSite Studio: first-client checklist

Goal: $5,000 gross revenue in six months. Planning target only: ten $500 projects.

Before public launch
- Confirm business name. Inquiry email: asimbzu@hotmail.com.
- Choose a payment method available in your country; test payment and withdrawal before invoicing.
- Test the email inquiry button in your browser and email app; it opens a draft addressed to asimbzu@hotmail.com.
- Confirm domain, hosting, delivery capacity, and written scope.
- Keep the demo labeled fictional. Do not claim clients or results you do not have.

First week
1. Review the website and demo; practice adapting the demo to one business.
2. Find 30 independent Dallas–Fort Worth cleaning businesses using their public websites.
3. Record business, website, public business contact, one observed website issue, and review date in prospects.csv.
4. Draft a personalized approach for each suitable prospect. Do not send automatically.
5. Aim to book one discussion and validate the $500 offer. Adjust after actual feedback.

Draft outreach (replace every bracket with verified information)
Subject: Website idea for [business]
Hi [name/team], I looked at [URL] and noticed [specific, accurate issue].
I build simple websites for cleaning businesses. My launch package is $500 for five pages, a quote-request form, and basic search setup; hosting and domain are separate.
Would you like to see a short design example for your business?
[Your name] | CleanSite Studio | asimbzu@hotmail.com

Suggested scope terms to agree with each client
- Exact pages, content responsibilities, hosting costs, and ownership.
- Delivery date begins after required content and access arrive.
- One consolidated revision round; additional work quoted separately.
- Suggested payment schedule: $250 to start, $250 after approval before launch; agree refund/cancellation terms in writing.
- No ranking, lead, or revenue guarantee.

Month targets: 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3 completed $500 projects.
Keep expenses and payment fees separate when measuring profit.
